# REMANSO — Art direction notes for the polish pass (written by the creative director after viewing all three builds)

The base (build C) is mechanically and technically the best, and it already ships the online mode. Visually it reads as a clean tech demo: flat water gradient, uniform white dots for motes, a thin plain circle for the remanso, faint sheen, flat sky. The client's first requirement is "incrivelmente lindo, cara de arte digital". Fix that here, with render-only changes (the sim must stay bit-identical: det.js and net.js must still pass), zero per-frame allocations, and the frame budget of GDD §5 (measure with stats().renderMs before/after on the same machine and keep the increase under ~1.5 ms at 1080p/rung 0).

Reference screenshots: build B's `shots/bot3/bot-act2.png` and `shots/set2/set-c0hold30.png` (dark water, streaked motes, dashed rim with beads, vignette) versus build C's `shots/final/04-*.png`, `08-*.png`, `11-*.png`.

## What must change (in priority order)
1. **Water is a place, not a gradient.** Deep indigo-black at the edges (#050814 → #0b1330 as specified, but with a slow large-scale luminance field on top: a seeded 2-octave value-noise tile (256×256, built once with the seeded PRNG, render-only) sampled per ink cell in the tone map and scrolled slowly with the mean flow, amplitude ~0.03). Calm sheen and the curl-iridescence must be VISIBLE: raise the sheen so eddies show cyan/magenta filaments where |curl| is high (it flips sign inside a remanso; that is the signature image of the game).
2. **Motes are plankton, not a starfield.** 900–1200 at rung 0, 1.5–2.5 px, alpha by speed (calm water: 0.10–0.20, fast: 0.45–0.7), warm-white tinted cyan in fast water; longer streaks (mote-layer fade 0.08–0.10 with the periodic destination-out purge) so vortices read as brushed silk like build B; dimmer inside calm regions so glyphs dominate; backward motes inside a remanso are brighter cyan and visibly counter-flowing.
3. **Ink is wet light.** Richer saturation at the core (C's cores are pale peach: reduce bloom over the core, mix toward the pure slot colour before the knee), a faint dark "wet edge" under each glyph (a 'multiply' or 'source-over' dark-indigo halo drawn from bloomA at ~0.25 alpha BEFORE the additive ink layer) so the glyph sits IN the water, not on it. Recalled ink flashes #bfe9ff exactly as specified.
4. **The remanso rim is the instrument.** Implement §5.2 row 9 fully and make it legible at a glance: dashed outer ring (counter-rotating dash offset), 24 strain segments coloured teal→amber→white with per-segment flicker at high strain, 12 beads at 0.82R that light with depth (the clock), soft inner glow disc, 24 sparks on close, P2 in rose. Build B's dashed ring is the right thickness (2 px) and spacing.
5. **Sky has depth.** Seeded static star field (150–250 tiny points, alpha 0.15–0.45, slow twinkle, render-only), a soft horizon glow (radial gradient centred on the horizon, ~6% alpha, colour of the act), the sky reflection band on the water as in §5.2 row 4 (30 wobbling slices, alpha fading with depth). Horizon is a 3-px soft gradient band, never a hard 1-px line. Constellations: stars with a soft radial glow sprite, 1.5-px lines at 0.18 alpha, sized so a 5-star constellation spans ~6–8% of the width.
6. **Dawn is the payoff.** Sky indigo → rose → gold band at the horizon (inverted gradient as specified), the FOSSIL layer visibly rising as gold strokes/rings on the water, mist rising (grey motes upward, 'lighter' 0.3), glyph tableau in their own colours, wisps in grey. It must look like a sunrise seen through water.
7. **Post:** vignette (source-over radial), grain 0.035 (rung 0). Keep. Text: check contrast in every act (dark warm text on the gold sky is correct).
8. **Lobby & indicators:** the "a dois" word and the two-dot link must be elegant: serif, letter-spaced, alpha 0.35 → 0.8 on hover; the lobby panel per GDD-ONLINE; the connected indicator two dots with a hairline. Remote player's cursor ring in rose, local in teal-white, both with the breathing radius.

## Checklist a judge will apply to your screenshots (1080p, setQuality('high'), act0 with remanso open, act2 with two colours + vortex, act3 storm, dawn at 20 s, epilogue, lobby open, online connected with two cursors)
- Would an art director mistake this for a frame from a shipped indie art game? (yes/no per screenshot)
- Are eddies visible as iridescent filaments? Is the reversed remanso visibly different (magenta sheen, cyan backward motes)?
- Is the rim readable as a clock and a strain meter?
- Does the sky have depth and does the water reflect it?
- Is anything harsh: hard edges, pure black, uniform white dots, 1-px lines, flat gradients, text without letter-spacing?
